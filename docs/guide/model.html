The radiometric camera model

The paper's model (§Image Formation, §Radiometric Camera Model), in the paper's symbols, which the code uses as identifiers.

The model

A sensor position 𝐩 ∈ 𝓘 = [0, W] × [0, H] (COLMAP pixels of the image, VisualGeometryToolkitCore's Rect(sensor)) has the chief ray π⁻¹(𝐩; Θ_d) of the geometric model. Its radiometric back-projection from the aperture point 𝐚 of the disc 𝒜 of radius r is

π̂⁻¹(𝐩, 𝐚) = (𝔣(𝐩) − 𝐚) / ‖𝔣(𝐩) − 𝐚‖,      𝔣(𝐩) = 𝔭 + t(𝐩) π⁻¹(𝐩)/‖π⁻¹(𝐩)‖,

with the focus point 𝔣 at the distance t(𝐩) along the unit chief direction (t̂ = 1/t a thin plate spline over the sensor, Θ_f its nodal values; 𝔭 the centre of projection). The radiant flux of pixel 𝐮 is

Φ_c(𝐮; Θ̄) = ∫_{𝓘_𝐮} V(𝐩; Θ_v) ∫_𝒜 L_c(π̂⁻¹(𝐩, 𝐚), π̂⁻¹(𝐩, 𝐚) − 𝐚; Θ_r) d𝐚 d𝐩,

evaluated with fixed quadrature rules on the pixel 𝓘_𝐮 and on the unit disc 𝒟 (𝐚 = r â, d𝐚 = r² dâ: the r-dependence of the domain sits in the integrand, so ∂Φ/∂r is an interior derivative) and a Monte-Carlo estimate of the radiance L_c with common random numbers. The radiometric model Θ_r: the vignetting V(𝐩) = Σ_i N_{i,3}(‖𝐩‖/r_max) P_i (a radial cubic B-spline, Θ_v = {P_i} with P_0 = P_1 so V is smooth at the centre, gauge V(0) = 1 held exactly: the centre's free direction is eliminated from the parametrization), the lighting L_c^SVSH on the sphere (𝔟, r_SH) around the board centre with spherical-harmonics coefficients Θ_l, the spatially varying Cook–Torrance SVBSDF f_c^CT (GGX D, height-correlated G₂, dielectric Fresnel F_c) with Θ_m = {α_¬ink, α_ink, η_¬ink, η_ink, μ} blended by the texture value, and the board warping h (a thin plate spline over the board 𝓑, Core's posed height field and its ray root).

Parameter sets: Θ_e poses, Θ_d geometric model, Θ_f focus, r aperture, Θ_i = Θ_d ∪ Θ_f ∪ {r}, Θ_c = Θ_e ∪ Θ_i, Θ_r = Θ_l ∪ Θ_m (and Θ_v), Θ̄ = all. The calibration minimises

𝐫(Θ̄) = ϕ − Φ(Θ̄)

by Levenberg–Marquardt (NLLSsolver.jl) with injected pathwise Jacobians: forward-mode dual numbers through the same quadrature that evaluates Φ (the derivative of the discretised objective), the linear inner maps (the bicubic blend of the grid model's nodes, the thin plate spline of t̂) multiplied with the dual sensitivities, analytic columns for the blocks Φ is linear in (Θ_v, Θ_l) or affine in (μ).

RadiometricCamera.RadiometricCamera — Module
RadiometricCamera

Radiometric camera calibration by inverse ray tracing in sensor × aperture space (the paper's model, in the paper's symbols).

A geometric back-projection π⁻¹(𝐩) (a VisualGeometryToolkitCore camera on the sensor's pixels 𝐩 ∈ [0, W] × [0, H], src/camera/) becomes the radiometric back-projection from the aperture disc 𝒜 of radius r, with the focus point 𝔣(𝐩); the radiant flux of a pixel is evaluated by fixed quadrature rules on the pixel 𝓘_𝐮 and on the unit disc with a Monte-Carlo radiance, and the calibration minimises 𝐫(Θ̄) over Θ̄ by Levenberg–Marquardt with injected pathwise Jacobians (forward-mode dual numbers through the same quadrature):

π̂⁻¹(𝐩, 𝐚) = (𝔣(𝐩) − 𝐚)/‖𝔣(𝐩) − 𝐚‖,   𝔣(𝐩) = 𝔭 + t(𝐩) π⁻¹(𝐩)/‖π⁻¹(𝐩)‖
Φ_c(𝐮)    = ∫_{𝓘_𝐮} V(𝐩) ∫_𝒜 L_c(π̂⁻¹(𝐩, 𝐚), π̂⁻¹(𝐩, 𝐚) − 𝐚) d𝐚 d𝐩
𝐫(Θ̄)      = (ϕ − Φ(Θ̄), √(100 Σϕ²/|Θ_v|) (V(0) − 1))

Data conventions

Pixel coordinates are COLMAP convention (pixel i spans [i, i+1), centre i + 0.5): every sensor position 𝐩 of the package — the cameras' K, the focus and vignetting splines, the masks, the detections — is in them (𝐩_of_pixel(u, v) of Julia's 1-based pixel (u, v)). Camera frame x right, y down, z forward; a View's pose is VisualGeometryToolkitCore's camera → world EuclideanMap (R = R_cw, t = the camera centre 𝐜; world = the board / session frame, metres). Photographs are RAW files (VisualGeometryToolkitFeatures' load_raw_mosaic, linearised under each photograph's sensor model: load_photo, photo_set) or 16-bit linear PNGs, read by VisualGeometryToolkitFeatures' load_linear_image (the option io.linearize = "on" decodes sRGB); board textures are read linearly too, as the renderers see them.
