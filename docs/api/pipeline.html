Pipeline and options

The refinement pipeline of bin/refine.jl: the sessions in, the options from TOML, the stages, the records out.

Loading

RadiometricCamera.GEOMETRY_IMAGES — Constant

The geometry images, the one vocabulary of every geometry stage's image key (the chrome detect, the front ends' decode, the image-region measurement; owner 2026-11-16: ONE geometric image for every detector): "raw_green" a RAW photograph's VisualGeometryToolkitFeatures bilinear green on the raw grid (raw_green_image: full resolution, the raw's COLMAP pixels, no map), "luma" a decoded image read as grey (its own pixels). The half-resolution green cells are no geometry image.

RadiometricCamera.load_photo — Method
load_photo(path; linearize = false) -> RawMosaic | AbstractArray

THE loader of a photograph, decided by the file (is_raw_file), never by a flag: a RAW file is Core's RawMosaic through Features' load_raw_mosaic (the one RAW path), a decoded image its linear array (Features' load_linear_image; linearize decodes sRGB). A RAW photograph is linear: linearize with one is an ArgumentError. What a photograph contributes is then decided by its TYPE (the observation model, observe).

RadiometricCamera.load_session — Method
load_session(records, image_dir; component = 1, lens = :camera, grid_size = (65, 50), focus_init = :thin_lens,
             image_distance = nothing, downsample = 8, texture_downsample = 8, sensor_width = 7mm, r₀ = 1.7e-3, linearize = true,
             focus_grid = 5, load_targets = true, raw_dir = nothing, lens_identity = nothing)

Board, camera parts and initial poses of a calibration session stored as VisualGeometryToolkitIO records (records: CaliBlobs' save_calibration, or its one-time migration of a BlobBoards session export, scripts/migrate_rc_session.jl):

  • the camera: load(BoundedCamera, camera.json) — K in COLMAP pixels, the image extent, e.g. BlobBoards' division camera — bounded by its Sensor (sensor_width over the record's extent) and moved to the scene's pixels when the photographs are box-averaged by downsample (K re-expressed under the box downsample: Core's Cameras.reframed_calibration by the similarity x ↦ x / f): lens = :camera that pixel camera, :grid the generic grid model of grid_size sampled from it (grid_model);
  • the board: the target of component component, load(Rig, targets_<component>.json) — Core's planar target with its Canvas and the CanvasTexture of the printed image, whose file (textures/<id>.png, beside the records) is read by Features' load_linear_image and box-averaged by texture_downsample — as the TexturedTarget of the scene, whose world frame is the target's (metres);
  • the poses: the frames of trajectory_<component>.json (load(Trajectory, …): target → camera per frame, the frames' image names), each photograph's camera → world pose (poses) its inverse (lengths in metres through FlexUnits);
  • the focus surface: focus_init = :board_center (the board centre is in focus: its mean axial distance d_c, the lens equation's image distance d_s = f·d_c/(d_c − f), the start plane t̂ᵢ = cos αᵢ/d_c), :thin_lens (thin_lens_focus_distance; t̂ ≡ 0 when it is Inf), :board (the mean principal-ray distance to the board plane) or a number (metres); image_distance overrides d_s (nothing: from focus_init, :board the conjugate of the board distance, or a number);
  • the targets: the photographs image_dir/<image> in frame order (sRGB-decoded when linearize, box-averaged by downsample); with load_targets = false only the first is read, for the image size;
  • the capture's camera settings (settings): with raw_dir, each frame's RAW file (raw_file: the image's stem, a RAW extension) read by Features' load_raw_metadata (the EXIF alone) into Features' extract_capture_settings — Core's CaptureSettings, frame index → exposure and lens settings, with the body and the lens (lens_identity where the caller states it: an adapted lens EXIF misnames); nothing without raw_dir. Reading the RAW files is Features' decoder (its LibRaw extension, loaded with this package).

RadiometricCamera.observe — Method
observe(set::PhotoSet, k, mask; name = basename(set.files[k])) -> (values, mask)

Photograph k of set (photo_set) under the compute mask, read once (load_photo) under its model:

  • RAW photosites: the photosites of the observation model's channel on the raw grid — the mask restricted to the usable photosites of that colour filter (photosite_mask: uncensored at the ADC floor and the white level) that its sensor model does not list as defects (Core's defect mask: masked, never interpolated); the values its linear view under the model (Core's linearview: the model's black subtracted — the file's, a measured channel black or a dark frame's per photosite). Under a noise with a variance law (a gain: PoissonGaussian, or a RelativeNoise) Core's uncertain image, each value with its variance, whose rows are then whitened (masked_observations); plain values otherwise. A mosaic whose size is not the mask's (the camera's sensor) is refused;
  • decoded images: the image itself (sRGB-decoded when the model's Decoded states it) under the mask; an image whose size is not the mask's is refused.

RadiometricCamera.photo_set — Method
photo_set(camera::CalibratedCamera, files; characterisation = nothing, lens = nothing, sensor_model = AutoSensorModel()) -> PhotoSet
photo_set(observation::ObservationModel, files; characterisation = nothing, lens = nothing) -> PhotoSet

THE entry point of photographs (owner 2026-11-15: "if a sensor was estimated with a model, then it must be used to predict the model"): the photographs files read exactly as the camera's — or the calibration's — observation model states, by dispatch on its types, never by a flag. characterisation is a LOCATION only (a path of the vgio/sensor_characterisation record); what it must be is the camera's statement. lens: the lens the config states (an adapted lens EXIF misnames; Features' extract_capture_settings). A camera's prediction reads them under sensor_model (prediction_observation: "off" reads RAW photosites without the record, with the loud warning).

  • SensorRecord, Photosites: RAW photographs only; characterisation required and its file's sha256 equal to the record's — another file is refused naming both. Each photograph's model is Core's resolver sensor_model(characterisation, capture, k) by its EXIF; a frame the record does not describe (another body, OtherBody; an ISO it holds no entry for, UnmatchedISO) is refused by name.
  • NoSensorRecord, Photosites: RAW photographs only, each under the file's black (Core's resolver without a characterisation, reason NoCharacterisation); a characterisation is refused: the camera was calibrated without one.
  • NoSensorRecord, Decoded: decoded images only, the absent model each; a RAW photograph is refused, so is a characterisation.

RadiometricCamera.photosite_mask — Method
photosite_mask(m::RawMosaic, channel::Symbol) -> BitMatrix

The usable photosites of ONE colour filter of the mosaic m on the raw grid (a RAW photograph's observations, observe): channel :R, :G or :B by Core's photosite_colors — 0 the red, 1 and 3 the two greens of every Bayer cell, 2 the blue — with the censored photosites dropped at both ends: the ADC floor (raw = 0) and the white level (Core's saturationview, raw ≥ white); a value below the black level is noise, not censored, and stays.

RadiometricCamera.raw_green_image — Method
raw_green_image([T = Float32], m::RawMosaic; sensor_model::SensorModel, device) -> Matrix{T}

The geometry image "raw_green" of the mosaic m (on the host): VisualGeometryToolkitFeatures' bilinear green on the raw grid under the frame's sensor_model — the image Features' detect_sfop(m::RawMosaic) and the seeded detect_foerstner(m, seeds) detect on (the chrome detect), computed on device by Features' one kernel (_bilinear_green, rawgreen.jl: a green photosite its own value, a red or blue one the mean of its usable green 4-neighbours; defects missing, saturation censored at 1). Linear values (Core's linearview under the model), in the raw's COLMAP pixels (the mosaic's `rawframe_map`: the identity as decoded); NaN where no usable green is in the 3×3 (a defect cluster). No second implementation: Features exports no name for the plane alone (a Features API is the follow-up), so this is the one caller outside its detectors.

RadiometricCamera.thin_lens_focus_distance — Method
thin_lens_focus_distance(lens, sensor, r₀; d_s = nothing) -> d  (metres; Inf when the rays are parallel)

The thin-lens construction, in the meridional plane (pinhole 𝔭 at the origin, aperture in z = 0, sensor at z = −d_s): from the sensor point under the aperture border (height r₀), intersect the chief ray through 𝔭 with the ray that runs parallel to the axis to the aperture border and from there through the focal point (0, f_m = f_px · pitch, the camera's focal length in pixels times the pixel pitch of VisualGeometryToolkitCore's sensor); with the intersection x, d = ‖x − 𝔭‖ cos α (α the chief ray's incidence) — the thin-lens conjugate d = f_m·d_s/(d_s − f_m). The image distance d_s (metres) is an input: the default (nothing), the pinhole model's sensor distance (= f_m), makes the two rays exactly parallel → d = Inf (focus at infinity, t̂ ≡ 0); any image distance d_s > f_m makes them intersect at a finite d.

RadiometricCamera.rc_backend — Method
rc_backend(backend; inline = true, fallback = true) -> KernelAbstractions.Backend

THE device resolution of RC and its drivers: backend a tag of BACKEND_TAGS or its type. CPUCompute is KernelAbstractions' CPU(); GPUCompute loads the GPU vendor packages (GPU_VENDOR_PACKAGES) and takes VisualGeometryToolkitCore's vendor-agnostic resolver afresh (reset_default_device!, then default_device: a functional GPU, else the CPU). With no functional GPU it falls back to the CPU with a loud warning, or refuses (fallback = false). inline: the device backend compiling every call inlined (always_inline), RC's kernels' fast build — Core's resolver's non-inlined Jacobian kernel needs twice the local memory (1.5x slower at jacobian_chunk 4, 4.4x on the flux). Called at top level by a driver, so its later calls run in the world the vendor package's load made.

RadiometricCamera.INAPPLICABLE_KEYS — Constant
INAPPLICABLE_KEYS

The keys a run's other options make unusable: (table, key) => why, key === nothing for every key of the table, why(opts) the reason the key cannot act (a string) or nothing where it applies. The reader refuses a key a config STATES where its reason holds (pipeline_options: the key and the reason named), and the writer (config_dict) leaves such a key out, so a run's record reads back.

source
RadiometricCamera.PIPELINE_PATH_KEYS — Constant

The keys (table.key) whose values name an INPUT file or directory of a run: what a driver resolves against its data root and records as the run's inputs. io.init is a model spec, lens:<path> or grid:<path>. Every String option of PipelineOptions is here, in PIPELINE_OUTPUT_KEYS or in _NOT_PATH_KEYS (the suite checks the three cover the options): a new string option has to be declared one or the other.

RadiometricCamera.boolean_switch_violations — Method
boolean_switch_violations(config_dirs; data = ()) -> Vector{String}

The lint of the switch convention: every .toml under config_dirs (recursively) parsed, each TOML boolean found at a key path (VisualGeometryToolkitCore's leaf paths, table.key, an array's element key[i]) that no regex of data matches whole — a boolean that is a switch, which is spelled "on" | "off" — is a finding "file:path = value". data declares the booleans that are data, not switches (a statement of fact, or a key of another package's schema that package reads). RadiometricCamera.pipeline_options — Method

The options of a refinement from a TOML file (or its parsed table). RadiometricCamera.read_switch — Function
read_switch(v, key, cases = SWITCH_SPELLINGS) -> value

The switch key of a config with TOML value v, read through its cases (word => value): a two-way switch "on" → true, "off" → false; a switch whose availability is a condition states its own cases, "auto" among them, each a concrete value (a type: camera.sensor_model's SENSOR_MODEL_TAGS), so "auto" is resolved here and the core code never sees a string. TOML's true/false is refused naming the new spelling (key = true → use key = "on"; RC 0.11.4, no alias), any other value refused naming the cases. THE ONE reader of every switch: the pipeline's options, the sensor model and the paper project's drivers.

The config spelling of the switch value x among cases (read_switch's inverse; the writer's): "on" / "off" for a Bool.

The pipeline

RadiometricCamera.measure_image_region — Method
measure_image_region(files; image, outlier_halfwidth_px, seed, device = default_device(), detect = detect_image_circle) -> NamedTuple

The image region of a capture set — where light falls on the sensor (the lens's lit region, an Ellipse in COLMAP pixels of the raw grid) — MEASURED on the set's own photographs files (RAW files, or full-resolution decoded .png), never predicted from a lens (a model's fold says where the model is valid, not where the field stop cuts the light): VisualGeometryToolkitFeatures' detect_image_circle (detect), streamed over the frames — the lens coverage's OUTER edge, the RANSAC ellipse (seeded by StableRNG(seed): the same region every run) through each angle's log-brightness drops on the frames' per-pixel maximum, resampled log-polar about the image centre on device. image is the geometry image measured on (one of GEOMETRY_IMAGES, the config's statement; no default): "raw_green" a RAW frame's bilinear green on the raw grid (raw_green_image, full resolution — the image every geometry detector reads, owner 2026-11-16), "luma" a decoded .png (a synthetic render) as stored, as Rec. 709 luma, on the raw grid. Both are on the raw grid (their frame map onto the raw is the identity), so the ellipse and outlier_halfwidth_px are raw pixels as given, nothing scaled. A "raw_green" set of decoded frames, a "luma" set of RAW frames or a mixed set is refused; no ellipse found is an error (the set's config must then state camera.lens_coverage = "sensor" or name other frames). Returns (; lens_coverage, extent, frames, center_scale, axes_scale, inliers, candidates, rim_gap, cut, seconds), the fields of the record (save_image_region). The measurement is GEOMETRIC (a rim threshold on log-brightness drops): its frames are read under the file's black, no sensor model — exempt from the observation model (RC 0.11.1; every photograph a camera is calibrated on or predicts goes through photo_set).

source
RadiometricCamera.run_lens_coverage — Method
run_lens_coverage(opts, W, H) -> Union{Nothing, Ellipse{Float64}}

The lens coverage of a run on W × H photographs, as its options state it (camera.lens_coverage): nothing for "sensor" (the sensor's rectangle, Core's default: the lens lights the whole sensor; nothing measured), else the measured image region of the rc/image_region/v1.0 record the option names (load_image_region: the capture set's keyed step) — refused when the record is missing or of other photographs (its extent not W × H). No other source: a grid start's stored coverage is not read.

RadiometricCamera.dataset_features — Method
dataset_features(path) -> [(image name, [(x, y, id)])]

The detections of a Schöps dataset.bin (a run's, or the texture's own detector run) in file order: COLMAP px (Schöps' pixel-corner convention is COLMAP's, SCHOEPS_PIXEL_FRAME), 0-based feature ids — VisualGeometryToolkitIO's load(TargetObservations, path; target); or a run's import, detections.json (schoeps_run_file: a vgio observations file in COLMAP px — v3.0, or an earlier target_points2d/v1 read through IO's version step — which names its targets itself, so IO's reader takes no target). The ids are one target's point indices: a file holding the detections of more than one target is refused (two targets' ids would collide).

RadiometricCamera.StartGeometry — Type
StartGeometry(camera, poses, observations, target; names, image_size = extent(camera), init_region = nothing, resample = nothing,
              features_for = _ -> observations, world = "the target's frame", page_flip = 1.0, board_y_sign = 1.0)

The geometric calibration a radiometric calibration starts from: the geometric camera (a BoundedCamera, the start camera), one poses[k] per photograph (camera → world, EuclideanMap), the observations[k] of the target in photograph k ((𝐩, X) pairs: the image point, COLMAP px, and the target point in the world, plain metres) and the printed target (a TexturedTarget). Records

RadiometricCamera's kinds, read and written through VisualGeometryToolkitIO (write_record, read_record), each nesting IO's records of the Core values it holds, under IO 0.10's envelope (one units and one conventions object): rc/radiometric_camera/v3.1 (a CalibratedCamera: the model, its observation model and its render settings), rc/radiometric_target/v2.0, rc/calibration_state/v3.1 (the state files state.json, state_it<N>.json and a start record's start state; with the photographs' observation model) and rc/run_parameters/v3.0. A record of an earlier RC version is refused by name — a camera or a state of RC 0.9–0.11.0 (v3.0) too, which states no observation model (RC 0.11.1: refused, never migrated, naming the refinement step that regenerates it) — but an rc/run_parameters/v2.0 (its grid prior* keys renamed to the smoothness vocabulary, RC 0.11.0), which reads through its version step. A camera's or a state's focus surface is the sensor-grid thin plate spline ("type": "sensor_thin_plate_spline"); a record of a removed focus surface (direction_thin_plate_spline, direction_spherical_spline) and a file of the removed kind rc/focus_ab (the focus A/B experiment's report) are refused by name.

The observation model and the one entry point of photographs

A calibrated camera states how its photographs were read (ObservationModel: the sensor characterisation's sha256 or none, RAW photosites or decoded images, the channel); every photograph a camera is calibrated on or predicts is read through photo_set / observe under it, by dispatch on its types (RC 0.11.1).
