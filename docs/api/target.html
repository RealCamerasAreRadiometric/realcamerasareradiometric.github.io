Target and lighting

The printed board 𝓑 and its warping h, the Cook–Torrance SVBSDF and the radiance L_c. The light is VisualGeometryToolkitCore's EnvironmentLight (real spherical harmonics per channel on a sphere about the board centre, SphericalHarmonicMap); its documentation is Core's.

The board and its warping

RadiometricCamera.PrintedTarget — Type
PrintedTarget{T}

The calibration board as the radiometry sees it: VisualGeometryToolkitCore's posed planar target (its geometry and design: target, whose canvas is the page; the quad its rays meet) with the printed appearance attached — the image's pixels, the optional distance map of the printed layout and its size in mm. See the file header for the chart convention. On a device the target itself stays behind (nothing): the kernels read only the quad and the appearance.

RadiometricCamera.sphere_radius — Method
sphere_radius(b::PrintedTarget, centres) -> r_SH

The pipeline's light-sphere radius about the board centre 𝔟: the farthest of the camera centres AND of the page's corners (sphere_radius(𝔟(b), centres) is the cameras alone). Every board point then lies inside the sphere, so every light ray from the page leaves it through the sphere (Core's exit_direction with Δ ≥ 0) — with cameras closer than the page's half-diagonal (the 27.10 set: 0.61 m against 0.71 m) the corners lay outside, where the exit falls back to the ray's closest point (2026-11-04).

RadiometricCamera.HEIGHT_NC — Constant
HEIGHT_NC = 25

The number of nodal heights of the board warping h (BoardSurface.h): Core's HeightField through a 5 × 5 node grid over the page, heights in world units along the board normal. Of these, HEIGHT_NFREE are free parameters.

source
RadiometricCamera.HEIGHT_NFREE — Constant
HEIGHT_NFREE = HEIGHT_NC - 3

The number of free parameters of the board warping h: the pure-bending nodal heights (Core's tangent_size of the warping's TargetManifold, height_basis, the same on a page of any aspect). The piston and the two tilts are the poses', so they are not parameters of h.

source
RadiometricCamera.INPLANE_NC — Constant
INPLANE_NC = 2

The number of parameters of the in-plane print error of the board (BoardSurface.inplane): the log-stretch a of Core's Aspect (x by eᵃ, y by e⁻ᵃ; a = 0.001 stretches x by 0.1 % against y) and the shear u of Core's Skew (x ↦ x + u y), both in the warping's placed chart (the page in units of its width, y up).

source
RadiometricCamera.BoardSurface — Type
BoardSurface{T}

The scene's surface parameters: the board warping h (the HEIGHT_NC nodal heights of the thin plate spline, board_height; all zero = the plane), the print's edge parameters edge = (δ [mm], σ_print [mm]) of a board with a distance map (PrintedTarget.sdf, edge_albedo; unused otherwise) and the in-plane print error inplane = (a, u) (inplane_calibration, Core's Aspect ∘ Skew; zero = the design as printed). BoardSurface() is the plane with no edge parameters, printed as designed.

RadiometricCamera.height_manifold — Method
height_manifold(b) -> VgtkCore.TargetManifold

The manifold of the board b's nodal heights: Core's TargetManifold of a HeightField over its page's warping_nodes. Core's thin-plate kinds are pure bending and have no gauge: the nodal heights move only within the heights whose interpolant on these nodes has no affine term (the piston and the two tilts are the poses'), so they are never parameters. The affine rows depend on the aspect, so each page has its own manifold.

intersect_board(b, 𝐨_ray, 𝐝, h) -> (s, 𝐱)

RadiometricCamera.posed_target — Method
posed_target(b, surface) -> VgtkCore.PosedTarget

The warped board as VisualGeometryToolkitCore's posed planar target: the page's target with the warping's HeightField (warping_field: the page in units of its width, origin at its top-left corner, y up) placed on the design by warping_placement — the parts of the render's surface (flat_surface, board_relief), so Core's surface of the result is the board's surface. RadiometricCamera.RadiometricTarget — Type
RadiometricTarget(target, warping, aspect, radiometry)

A calibration target as a refinement knows it, parallel to the geometric camera ↔ the RadiometricCameraModel: the geometric target (target, Core's AbstractTarget as the session's target record holds it, so its content identity is the session's; nothing for a state converted from a RadiometricCamera 0.5 .jls file, which did not record it), the board's warping as the refinement learned it (warping, the HEIGHT_NC nodal heights of BoardSurface.h, plain metres along the board normal — a calibration of the target that its content identity does not cover, so not the target's own HeightField), the aspect (height / width) of the page the warping was learned on (aspect, warping_aspect of its board: the same nodal heights are another surface on a page of another aspect), the in-plane print error as the refinement learned it (inplane, the (a, u) of Core's Aspect ∘ Skew in the warping's placed chart — inplane_calibration; zero = the design as printed) and the print's radiometry (TargetRadiometry). A flat warping (every nodal height zero) is the plane on any page: its aspect may be nothing; a warped one, or an in-plane error (whose chart depends on the aspect), without an aspect is an ArgumentError. board_surface is the scene's BoardSurface of it on a board.

TargetRadiometry(material, surface::BoardSurface)

The radiometry of a printed calibration target: its SVBSDF (material, a Material: Θm = the roughnesses and refractive indices of ink and paper and the diffuse weight μ) and, for a design given as a distance map, its printed edge — the ink spread δ (`inkspread, a length: a FlexUnits quantity in metres) and the print's own blur (printblur, Core'sGaussianBluron the print, round, its moment in m²); a plain ink spread or a plain (px²) print blur is anArgumentError. From a state's working form,materialand the surface'sedge = (δ, σprint)in mm, read decimal-exactly (VisualGeometryToolkitIO'sreadlength):edgeparameters(r)` gives the mm values back bit for bit for values of up to 15 significant digits.
